Project Edix

Securing academic records with blockchain.

The Problem

Primary and secondary schools face two related risks: grades can be altered directly in a school's database, and digital certificates issued to students are hard for anyone outside the school to verify as genuine.

Current State: Deployed on Testnet

Edix is deployed and in real use — schools are issuing credentials through it today, not just running it as a prototype. It's currently running on Cardano's preprod (testnet) network, not mainnet yet, so the ADA fees involved are test tokens, not real money.

The Solution

I designed a school management platform that integrates Cardano blockchain features directly into the database architecture. Each school gets its own sovereign identity (DID) and wallet. When a certificate is issued, its hash (not the certificate content itself) is written on-chain as a transaction, one per certificate — so anyone can verify a certificate is genuine by re-hashing it and checking the result against the chain, without exposing student data on a public ledger. Grades keep no chain footprint until a school actually issues a certificate.

Cardano was chosen over other chains mainly for transaction cost: issuing one hash per certificate at scale needs fees low enough that per-student cost stays negligible on mainnet, which rules out most higher-fee chains. Testing on preprod first lets that cost assumption get validated before any real ADA is spent.

Project Edix

Securing academic records with blockchain.

The Problem

Primary and secondary schools face two related risks: grades can be altered directly in a school's database, and digital certificates issued to students are hard for anyone outside the school to verify as genuine.

The Solution

I designed a school management platform that integrates Cardano blockchain features directly into the database architecture. Each school gets its own sovereign identity (DID) and wallet; certificate verification is anchored to the decentralized ledger, so records are immutable and can't be tampered with by editing a local database alone.
